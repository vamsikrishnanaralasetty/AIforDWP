# VPN Issue – Team Update

Good news — the VPN issue is resolved and your data was never at risk. When Windows 11 updated on your machine, it removed the old VPN app and the replacement didn't install automatically as it should have. We've pushed the fix to affected devices and confirmed connectivity is back to normal. If your VPN stops working again, please restart your device first — if that doesn't help, contact the service desk and reference ticket T-1008.
