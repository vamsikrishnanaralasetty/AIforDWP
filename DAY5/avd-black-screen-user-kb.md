# My Screen is Black After Signing In – Quick Fix

**Version:** v 1.0  
**Date:** 07/08/2026  
**Status:** Draft

---

## What's happening?

After you sign in to your work computer, you may see a black screen and find yourself unable to use your desktop. This is usually caused by a recent system update that's not compatible with your graphics settings. **Don't worry — your data is completely safe and hasn't been affected.**

---

## What you can do right now

If you're seeing a black screen after signing in, try these simple steps:

### Step 1: Sign out completely
- **Hold down** `Ctrl + Alt + Delete` on your keyboard
- Click **Sign out** or **Log off**
- Wait for the screen to go dark and show the login screen again

### Step 2: Wait
- After signing out, wait a full 30 seconds before doing anything else
- This gives the system time to reset

### Step 3: Sign back in
- Click on your name or enter your username and password as normal
- Your desktop should load properly this time

---

## If it doesn't work

If the black screen problem happens again:

1. **Repeat Steps 1–3 above** one more time
2. **Wait 2–3 minutes** — the IT team is working on a permanent fix
3. If you still can't use your desktop, **contact the Service Desk immediately** (see below)

---

## What if I need to use my computer right now?

You can still work using a different computer while this is being fixed. Ask your manager or the Service Desk for a loaner device, or use a colleague's device if available.

---

## Contact the Service Desk if:

- The black screen appears even after you sign out and wait 30 seconds
- You can't sign back in after trying these steps more than twice
- The problem keeps happening

---

## Details to mention in your ticket:

When you call or email the Service Desk, please mention:

- **Approximate time** you first saw the black screen (e.g., "around 9:00 AM")
- **How many times** you've tried signing out and back in
- **Your department** (e.g., Finance, HR, Operations)
- If this happened **right after a system restart**
- **Your computer name** (you can find this by right-clicking "This PC" → Properties and looking for "Device name")

---

## Your data is safe

Even though you can't see your desktop, all of your files, emails, and documents are secure and stored safely. Nothing has been deleted or lost. Our IT team is working on getting you back up and running quickly.
