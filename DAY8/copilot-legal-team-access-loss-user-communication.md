## What is happening?

A number of you have reported — and our own monitoring has confirmed — that Copilot is currently unavailable across the Legal department. This appears to have begun this morning and affects everyone on the team.

We understand how important Copilot has become to your day-to-day work, and we recognise the impact this outage is having at a busy time.

---

## What we know so far

- The issue affects the Legal team specifically and appears to have started this morning
- Copilot was working normally for everyone last week
- The sudden, team-wide nature of this strongly suggests a technical configuration issue rather than anything affecting individual accounts

We are actively investigating and have already begun checking our systems to identify the cause. We will share more information as soon as we have it.

---

## What we are doing right now

Our team is working through the following steps as a priority:

1. Checking whether there is a known service issue on Microsoft's side that may be affecting Copilot availability
2. Reviewing account and licensing configurations for the Legal team
3. Identifying whether any system changes made recently may have inadvertently affected your access
4. Working to restore access as quickly as possible once the cause is confirmed

---

## What you should do in the meantime

- **No action is required from you** to resolve this — the fix will be applied on the IT side
- If you need to complete urgent work that you would normally use Copilot for, please use the standard Microsoft 365 tools (Outlook, Word, SharePoint) directly in the meantime
- If you experience any **other** Microsoft 365 issues beyond Copilot (such as problems with email, Teams, or SharePoint), please let us know separately as this may help us diagnose the issue more quickly

---

We are not yet able to confirm a precise resolution time, but we are treating this as our highest priority today. We will send a further update within **one hour** with either a confirmed resolution or a revised timeline.

We ask for your patience while we work through this — we are committed to restoring your access as quickly as possible.
