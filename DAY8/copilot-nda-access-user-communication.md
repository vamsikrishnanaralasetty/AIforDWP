## What happened?

When you asked Copilot to summarise the client NDA stored in SharePoint, it responded with: *"I don't have access to that content."*

We want to reassure you — this is not a fault with Copilot, and it is not something you did wrong.

---

## Why couldn't Copilot access the document?

Microsoft 365 Copilot works within the same permissions system as the rest of Microsoft 365. This means **Copilot can only read and summarise documents that you already have permission to open yourself.**

In this case, you heard about the document in a meeting, but your account had not yet been granted access to the folder where it is stored. Because of this, Copilot was blocked from reading it — just as you would be if you tried to open the file directly.

This is a deliberate and important security feature. It ensures that Copilot cannot expose confidential or restricted documents to anyone who has not been formally given permission to see them.

---

## What should you do next?

Here are the steps to get this sorted:

1. **Contact the document owner** — Reach out to the person who manages the folder or the SharePoint site where the NDA is stored (your manager or a colleague from the meeting may be able to point you in the right direction).

2. **Request access** — Ask them to grant your account permission to view the document. This is a straightforward process for a SharePoint site owner or document owner.

3. **Try Copilot again** — Once access has been granted and you can open the document yourself, please allow up to 24 hours and then try your Copilot request again. It should work as expected at that point.

4. **Still having trouble?** — If you run into any difficulties or access is not granted within a reasonable timeframe, please reply to this message or raise a new support ticket and we will be happy to help.

---

## A quick note about Copilot and document access

As a general rule: if you can open a document yourself, Copilot can help you with it. If you cannot open it, neither can Copilot. This keeps your organisation's information safe and ensures the right people have access to the right content.
