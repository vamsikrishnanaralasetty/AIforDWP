## What is happening?

When you ask Copilot in Outlook to find or summarise case emails, it is returning no results.

We want to reassure you: **this is almost certainly not a fault with Copilot.** It is a normal part of how new Microsoft 365 accounts are set up, and it is something that resolves on its own within a short time.

---

## Why can't Copilot find the emails?

There are two likely reasons, and both are straightforwardly related to your account being new:

**1. Your mailbox is still being set up in the background**  
When a new account is created, Microsoft 365 needs time to organise and catalogue all of your emails so that tools like Copilot can search through them. Think of it like a new filing system that needs a day or two to get everything into the right folders before it can help you find things. This process typically takes **24 to 72 hours** from when your account was first created.

**2. Some emails may not be in your personal mailbox yet**  
If the case emails you need were sent before you joined — for example, earlier correspondence between colleagues — they would not automatically appear in your inbox. Those emails may be stored in a shared team mailbox or a case inbox that you may need to be given access to separately.

---

## What should you do next?

Here are some simple steps to try:

1. **Give it a little time** — If your account was set up this week, please try again in 24–48 hours. Copilot should start finding your emails once the background setup is complete.

2. **Open the key emails yourself first** — If colleagues have forwarded case emails to you, try opening them directly in Outlook. This can help the system recognise them more quickly.

3. **Ask about shared mailboxes** — If the case history you need lives in a team inbox or a shared mailbox, please speak to your line manager or the case owner. They can arrange for you to be given access, and once that is in place, Copilot will be able to help you with that content too.

4. **Check back with us if it is still not working after 72 hours** — If Copilot still cannot find your emails by the end of the week, please reply to this message or raise a new support ticket. We will investigate further and make sure everything is set up correctly for you.

---

## A note on what Copilot can and can't do

Copilot is a brilliant tool once your account is fully set up, but it can only work with emails and documents that are already in your mailbox and that you have permission to access. It cannot reach back into history before you joined, and it will not show you content stored elsewhere unless you have been given access to it.

This is by design — it keeps your organisation's information secure and ensures the right people see the right content.
