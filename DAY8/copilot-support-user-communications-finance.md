## Ticket 1 — Finance lead: Copilot won't summarise the Q3 board pack

**Subject:** Your Copilot question about the board pack — update from IT

Hi,

Thanks for getting in touch. We've looked into why Copilot couldn't summarise the Q3 board pack for you.

There are two likely explanations, and neither means anything is wrong with your access or your account:

- **If the file was uploaded or moved recently:** Copilot needs a short period — usually up to 24 hours — to make newly added files available for summarising. If the board pack was added in the last day or so, try again today and it should work.
- **If the file has a security classification label applied:** Some of our most sensitive documents are protected in a way that deliberately prevents automated tools, including Copilot, from reading their contents. This is intentional — it keeps the most confidential material under tighter control. If this applies to the board pack, you would need to open and read it directly rather than via Copilot.

**What to do next:** Please try again now. If Copilot still can't summarise it, reply to this message and we'll confirm which of the above applies and advise on your options.

Nothing is wrong with your account and no action is needed from you in the meantime.

## Ticket 3 — HR manager: Copilot said it doesn't have access to a salary review spreadsheet

**Subject:** Copilot access to the salary review file — explanation and next steps

Hi,

Thanks for letting us know. The message Copilot showed you — *"I don't have access to that content"* — is actually working as intended in this case, and it's good news from a security perspective.

The salary review spreadsheet has a security protection applied to it that prevents automated tools, including Copilot, from reading its contents. This protection exists specifically for documents like payroll and salary data to make sure that sensitive information can only be accessed directly by authorised people — not processed or summarised by any automated system.

This means your data is safe and the protection is doing its job.

**What to do next:** To work with the file, please open it directly in Excel as you normally would. Copilot won't be able to assist with the contents of that specific document, but you have full access to it yourself. If you need IT to confirm your access is correct, just reply and we'll check.

## Ticket 4 — Sales rep: Copilot can't find a client contract shared from another company

**Subject:** Copilot and files shared from outside our organisation

Hi,

Thanks for getting in touch. We've looked into why Copilot couldn't find the client contract, and the reason is straightforward.

Copilot for Microsoft 365 can only search and work with files that are stored within our own organisation's systems. The contract you're looking for was shared with you from the client's own Microsoft 365 environment — it lives on their systems, not ours. Copilot has no way to reach across into another company's storage, and this is by design to protect everyone's data.

**What to do next:** If you need to use Copilot to work with this contract — for example to summarise it or draft a response — the simplest solution is to save a copy of the document into your own OneDrive or into the relevant SharePoint folder in our environment. Once it's saved here, Copilot will be able to access it.

Please make sure you're allowed to save a local copy under the terms of your agreement with the client before doing so. If you're unsure, check with your manager or the Legal team.

IT Support

## Ticket 8 — Executive assistant: Copilot can't see the shared mailbox calendar

**Subject:** Copilot and shared mailbox calendars — current limitation

Hi,

Thanks for raising this. We've looked into it and want to be upfront with you: this is a current limitation of Copilot for Microsoft 365, not something that can be fixed with a configuration change right now.

Copilot in Outlook is currently designed to work with your own personal mailbox and calendar. It doesn't yet have the ability to see or work with shared mailboxes or calendars that you manage on behalf of someone else — even when you have full delegate access to them. This applies to your director's calendar and any shared mailboxes you look after.

Microsoft has this on their roadmap to improve, and we'll let you know as soon as it becomes available.

**What to do next:** For now, please continue managing the shared mailbox and calendar directly in Outlook as you normally would. Copilot can still help you with your own emails, meeting prep for meetings in your own calendar, and drafting — it's just this one area it can't assist with yet.

We'll keep an eye on Microsoft's updates and reach out when this changes.

IT Support
