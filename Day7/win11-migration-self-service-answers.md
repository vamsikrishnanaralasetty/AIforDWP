# Win11 Migration – Self-Service Answers

---

## My account is locked and I can't log in

Wait 15 minutes and try logging in again — accounts unlock automatically after a short time. When you try again, type your password carefully (check Caps Lock is off).

If it locks again after you get back in, change your password straight away: press **Ctrl + Alt + Delete** and choose **Change a password**.

**Still locked or keeps happening?** Call the Service Desk. Tell them: your full name, your username, roughly what time it locked, and how many times this week it has happened.

---

## My OneDrive files seem to be missing

Click the **OneDrive cloud icon** in the bottom-right of your screen. If it shows a red X or warning, click it and choose **Resume syncing**. Wait two minutes and check your files again.

If the icon isn't there, restart your computer — OneDrive should reconnect on its own.

**Files still missing after a restart?** Contact the Service Desk straight away. Tell them: your name, which folder or file is missing, and whether you have a deadline today — this helps them prioritise your case.

---

## The floor 3 printer isn't showing up

Try disconnecting and reconnecting to the network: click the Wi-Fi or network icon in the bottom-right corner, disconnect, then reconnect. Sign out of your computer and sign back in — the printer should map automatically on login.

In the meantime, the printer on **floor 2** is available to use.

**Printer still not showing after signing back in?** Contact the Service Desk. Tell them: your name, your computer name (shown on the sticker on your laptop or desktop), and that you are on floor 3. An engineer will need to come to the floor to fix this.
