# Win11 Migration — Executive Summary

**For:** Senior Leadership  
**Prepared by:** DWP Analyst  
**Date:** 2026-08-12  

---

The Windows 11 migration for the FinBridge staff has largely been a success. The majority of users are now working on faster, more reliable machines and have reported a noticeably better experience compared to before the migration. The issues that emerged in the first week — users being locked out of their accounts, files appearing to go missing, and the VPN dropping during calls — were all identified quickly and resolved within the week. Staff confirmed these fixes in their own words, and no further reports of those problems have come in. Login speeds, file access, and remote connectivity are all back to normal or better than before.

Two issues remain open and are being actively worked. The printer on the third floor has not mapped correctly since the migration began, leaving that team reliant on walking to another floor to print client documents. This is unacceptable three weeks in and an engineer is being assigned to resolve it on-site with a firm deadline. Separately, a security improvement applied to Finance staff machines last week has had an unintended side effect: computers are taking noticeably longer to be ready to use each morning. The security change itself was the right thing to do, and the protection it adds is being kept — but the team is working to adjust how it runs so it no longer slows down the start of the working day. Both issues are understood, owned, and being resolved. No data has been lost and no security risk exists.
